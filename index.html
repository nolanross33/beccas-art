About Becca

Hi, I'm Becca — an artist based in Dallas, TX with a love for capturing the details that make a place or a pet uniquely theirs.

I work primarily in watercolor and gouache, with each piece hand-painted to order. Whether it's the way your dog tilts their head or the worn brick of a building you love, I aim to translate those personal moments into something you'll treasure forever.

Every commission starts with a conversation. I take the time to understand what you want to capture before I put brush to paper.
